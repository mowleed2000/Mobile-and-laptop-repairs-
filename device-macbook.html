Apple MacBook repair

MacBook repair in West Kensington

Cracked MacBook lid, a swollen trackpad, liquid on the keys, or a USB-C port that will not charge. We diagnose that Air or Pro on North End Road, then quote. Newer machines have soldered storage — we will be straight about what can be upgraded.

Common faults

What we see most on this MacBook

Tell us the fault on the form. These are the jobs we see most on this device.

Cracked screen

Lines, ink spots or a black lid. Panel is quoted for that MacBook after we see it.

Battery / swollen trackpad

Only runs on the charger, or the trackpad is lifting. Stop using a swollen pack and bring it in.

Keyboard, HDMI and USB-C

Sticky keys or a port that will not charge or drive a monitor. Isolated on the bench before we quote.

Liquid spill

Tea or water on the keys. Leave it off — do not try to charge it wet. Cleaning is quoted after we open it.
